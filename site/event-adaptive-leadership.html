About the programme

Leadership used to rest on expertise and predictability. Today the edge comes from adapting, learning and responding to constant change.

This two-day workshop introduces the Adaptive Capability Framework with practical tools, group exercises and reflection for leading through uncertainty.

  1. Why disruption demands a different leadership approach
  2. The core capabilities for adapting with confidence
  3. Applying the Adaptive Capability Framework to real challenges
  4. Spotting the opportunities and risks change creates
  5. A personal action plan for team and organisational adaptability

Your speaker

Chris Nel

An internationally recognised expert on Adaptive Leadership and co-founder of Quest Leadership.

  • One of four Europe-based Master Facilitators of The Leadership Challenge®
  • Former British Army officer and senior executive in two FTSE 100 companies
  • Has worked with Rolls-Royce, Hitachi, Lufthansa, Virgin Money and the UK Ministry of Defence
  • Helps organisations build adaptive capability through change and transformation
