About the programme

HEC returns for its 8th edition, bringing international speakers, authors, executives and HR leaders together around one question: how do people, purpose and performance thrive together?

Keynotes, case studies and panels give participants proven strategies for healthier cultures, stronger leadership and higher engagement.

  1. Learn from renowned speakers and workplace experts
  2. Build a culture of wellbeing and high performance
  3. Lead with trust, resilience and purpose
  4. Improve employee experience and retention
  5. Connect with executives shaping the future of work
