About the programme

Constant notifications and digital noise make it hard to focus on what matters, at work and at home.

Alexander Bell shows leaders and teams how to build Attention Intelligence®: staying focused, intentional and resilient in a connected world.

  1. The 7 rules behind our tech habits
  2. The science linking attention, leadership and productivity
  3. Practical strategies for digital focus
  4. Running a personal Attention Audit™
  5. Tech tips and a tool to multiply productivity

Your speaker

Alexander Bell

A global keynote speaker on technology, productivity and human connection.

  • Creator of the Attention Intelligence® and Tech Rules® frameworks
  • Serial entrepreneur: built his first company into a PLC by his twenties
  • Partnered with Google to bring Street View indoors
  • Led VR studio Reality Media Group, working with clients such as Warner Bros.
