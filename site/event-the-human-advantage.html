About the programme

AI is changing how we work, but it cannot replace what makes leaders exceptional: confidence, purposeful communication and real relationships.

Deborah Henley combines behavioural insight and practical technique to strengthen the human side of leadership.

  1. Leading with confidence and authenticity
  2. Communicating with clarity that drives action
  3. Building trust and stronger relationships
  4. Influence without relying on authority
  5. Resilience and decisions under pressure
  6. A culture where people do their best work

Your speaker

Deborah Henley

An executive coach and award-winning speaker on leadership communication and emotional intelligence.

  • Creator of the REAL Leadership Framework for leading in an AI-driven world
  • Author of Your Leadership Story, a Business Book Awards finalist
  • Trusted by Microsoft, Accenture, Cisco, Goldman Sachs and the UK MOD
  • Blends psychology, neuroscience and storytelling
