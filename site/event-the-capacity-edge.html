About the programme

Leadership is tested in moments of uncertainty and sustained pressure, when staying clear and steady is hardest.

Sanita Pukite introduces The Capacity Edge®, a framework for expanding a leader’s capacity to handle pressure and lead with confidence.

  1. Leadership capability versus leadership capacity
  2. The hidden factors that erode effectiveness under pressure
  3. Resilience, decisions and presence in uncertainty
  4. Strategies to expand personal capacity
  5. An action plan for leading teams with clarity and purpose

Your speaker

Sanita Pukite

An executive coach and leadership development expert focused on performance and resilience.

  • Creator of The Capacity Edge® framework
  • INSEAD faculty member and former leader of its Women in Business Club
  • Former Johnson & Johnson EMEA senior leader
  • Author of the forthcoming book The Capacity Edge
